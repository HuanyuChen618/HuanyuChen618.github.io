Our photos on the sky

Every night-sky photo we matched to a star catalogue, placed where it was pointing. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a photo to read about that night.

Inside view: as seen from the ground, north celestial pole up. Outside view: a celestial globe, which is mirror-reversed because you look at the sky from beyond it. Lines: Stellarium modern sky culture. Stars: HYG catalogue.
